SOLFARER

wander the real stars

Every point of light is a real star system — 2,236 of them within 100 light-years of Sol, from the Hipparcos catalog, in their true colors. Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, click a star to read its card, and SET COURSE to go there.

Travel is honest special relativity: a 1g burn to the midpoint, flip, and brake. The universe ages faster than you do — that's not a game mechanic, it's the twin paradox. Your log keeps the lifetime score.

AUTO TRAVEL skips the flight and arrives instantly. Leave it off to ride along: the star field ahead blueshifts, crowds forward with aberration, and at high gamma the cosmic microwave background itself glows ahead of you. All of it from the real physics.

Zoom out and the bubble shrinks to a huddle of everything you can reach in a human lifetime — while the famous beacons hang far beyond: Betelgeuse, Rigel, Polaris, Deneb. They're reachable too. Check what the trip costs before you go.

The exploration sibling of Lighthaul, the relativistic courier game. Data: HYG / Hipparcos. Click anywhere to close.
